# Replay fixed-array copy-loop certificates

## Problem

The fixed-size `fill3` mdtest now uses the frontier-local `loop` tactic. Its
quantified written-prefix invariant, concrete post-loop instantiations, and
generated certificate all replay.

The corresponding `copy3` migration remains blocked. A sound frontier proof
must carry an inductive relation between the written destination prefix and
the unchanged source snapshot. With fixed-size array parameters, preserving
that relation across `dst[i] = src[i]` can succeed during smart reasoning but
fail while lowering the result to Surface Click:

```text
surface premises do not replay the atomic derivation
unexpressed derivation premises: CMemoryMutatesOnly ... has no replayable
Surface Click spelling at this proof point
```

This is a tooling blocker, not permission to change either `int32 dst[3]` or
`int32 src[3]` to an unsized pointer contract, concretely unroll the C loop,
or replace the copy with proof-friendly C. Fixed-size parameters additionally
materialize their cells, which is the relevant boundary.

## Minimal regression

Use the unchanged `copy3.c` from `mdtests/copy3_array_demo.md`: two disjoint
`int32[3]` parameters, an index starting at zero, and a loop storing
`dst[i] = src[i]`. A frontier-local proof may carry either a quantified copied
prefix or three guarded cell equalities. Its preservation proof executes the
store and increment and then closes the invariants.

The copied-cell derivation may depend on the certified store equation, the
store's checked mutation footprint, destination loadability, and framing of
the disjoint source cell. Certificate lowering must recognize those as
replay-generated evidence instead of demanding impossible user-written
spellings for internal facts. `close_invariants()` must remain a deterministic
simple tactic; this issue is not permission to turn it into another smart
search or to add arbitrary candidate limits.

## Acceptance criteria

- The fixed-array `copy3` frontier-loop proof verifies without C or signature
  changes and without concrete loop unrolling.
- Smart invariant closure emits a simple certificate that freshly replays.
- `click expand` and `click audit` accept every migrated smart site.
- Internal mutation summaries remain non-assumable; they are available only
  when regenerated by the exact preceding statement certificate.
- The fixed-array `fill3` and runtime-sized array invariant regressions
  continue to pass promptly.

# Replay fixed-array loop invariant certificates

## Problem

Migrating the fixed-size `copy3` and `fill3` mdtests to the frontier-local
`loop` tactic exposes an expansion/certificate disagreement.  The invariant
closer can derive preserved fixed-array cells after an indexed store, using
the step's checked `CMemoryMutatesOnly` fact, but lowering that successful
derivation to Surface Click fails:

```text
surface premises do not replay the atomic derivation
unexpressed derivation premises: CMemoryMutatesOnly ... has no replayable
Surface Click spelling at this proof point
```

This is a tooling blocker, not permission to change `int32 p[3]` to an
unsized pointer contract or concretely unroll the C loop.  The corresponding
runtime-sized array invariant works; fixed-size parameters additionally
materialize their cells, which is the relevant boundary.

## Minimal regression

Use unchanged C with an `int32 p[3]` parameter, an index starting at zero,
and a loop storing `p[i] = i`.  A frontier-local proof carries either the
quantified filled-prefix invariant or three guarded cell invariants.  Its
preservation proof executes the store and increment and then closes the
invariants.

The kernel derivation may depend on the certified store effect produced by
the preceding simple `step()`.  Certificate lowering must recognize that as
replay-generated evidence instead of demanding an impossible user-written
surface premise.

## Acceptance criteria

- The fixed-array `fill3` and `copy3` frontier-loop proofs verify without C or
  signature changes and without concrete loop unrolling.
- Smart invariant closure emits a simple certificate that freshly replays.
- `click expand` and `click audit` accept every migrated smart site.
- Internal mutation summaries remain non-assumable; they are available only
  when regenerated by the exact preceding statement certificate.
- The runtime-sized array invariant regressions continue to pass promptly.
